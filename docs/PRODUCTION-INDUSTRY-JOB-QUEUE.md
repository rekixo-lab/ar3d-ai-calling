# Purpose-aware production calling preparation queue

This adds a distinct tenant-scoped CRM queue for an exact reviewed School campaign, immutable recipient review revision/expiry, frozen graph digest and approved School audio manifest digest. It does not invent property campaigns. Client admins may prepare at most 50 selected currently cleared recipients in one all-or-none statement, after actual R2 verification. A single prepared job per tenant/lead and a permanent unique campaign/lead/review revision prevent duplicate preparation; cancel before another review is prepared. Admins with current MFA can inspect/cancel, and client admins can cancel their own company's prepared jobs. Read-only history returns IDs and state, not destination numbers or transcripts. Deletion readiness counts the additional dependency.

State is strictly prepared/cancelled; SQL triggers forbid in-place scope changes, deletion or any live state. Archived campaigns, revoked/expired reviews, changed lead, suppression, suspension or invalid audio fail the server-only readPreparedIndustryJob freshness contract. List status is local evidence freshness plus recorded audio approval, not a byte-integrity guarantee. Cross-D1 reads cannot be atomic; a future funded claim must recheck after reservation and before external submission. No current scheduler consumes these records. A later explicit release/claim migration and shared capacity, money, carrier, CDR and results adapter are required; old prepared records must not auto-dial on rollout. No billing or caller configuration changes. The additive CRM migration requires the complete prior Control schema, including 0038 audio. No separate environment or real provider request.
