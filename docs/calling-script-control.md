# Company information and draft scripts

Under **Client Company & AI Setup**, a current MFA Super Admin can review a company's knowledge, preview a template and save versions of its script. Clients can view their own company information and send requests for changes. Editing drafts records a new version and does not change the fixed questionnaire.

To change the phone questions, update and verify the fixed questionnaire and its recorded prompts. See [the phone setup guide](scripted-phone-pilot.md).
