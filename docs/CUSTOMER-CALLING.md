# Customer campaigns

Complete the [verified-number pilot](scripted-phone-pilot.md) before enabling customer traffic. In production, use the exact custom hostname for the signed callbacks. Set `AR3D_SCRIPTED_CAMPAIGNS_ENABLED=enabled` only after the operator approves the carrier account, caller, permitted purpose and recording configuration.

## Company setup

1. Upload the reviewed Hindi or English MP3 prompts to `scripted-phone-campaigns/<tenant-uuid>/<hi-or-en>/` in private R2. Use the [pilot filenames](scripted-phone-pilot.md) and identify the calling company and purpose in its opening. The opening explains recording and asks recipients to press 1 to continue or 2 to stop further calls. Keep every prompt under 512 KiB.
2. Under **Calling Setup → Client calling approval**, a current MFA Super Admin saves the provider approval reference, an approval of up to 365 days, the 180–300 second call limit and the **Customer consent** mode. With **Client declares consent** (recommended), the Client Admin chooses where the customers came from and confirms consent when pressing **Start calling**; every eligible customer in that campaign is cleared with that declaration (`client-declared:<source>:<time>`, audit `calling.consent_declared`). Opted-out, denied and DND-blocked customers are never cleared. With **AR3D reviews each customer**, use **Customer clearance** with consent evidence and a current DND reference as before. There is no per-company daily cap: prepaid minutes and provider ceilings limit volume.
3. From **Customers / Excel**, the client uploads a CSV or XLSX with **up to 1,000 unique customer numbers per file** (header excluded), previews it, enters a campaign name and selects its language. A campaign holds up to 2,000 customers. A single number can also be typed under **Add one customer manually** and added to an existing or new *Manual numbers* campaign. **Import & prepare campaign** creates a draft and opens **Calling**; nothing is dialed until **Start calling**.
4. In **Results / CRM**, select the campaign and refresh. Each call's normalized answers appear beside its outcome. **Saved to lead** confirms the CRM update. **Answers captured · lead needs review** preserves valid answers for follow-up when a staff edit or lead restriction prevented an automatic CRM update.

## Fast CRM follow-up actions

- Client Admin → **Customers / Excel → Customer list → Interested leads** now selects the existing Interest status filter immediately (read-only shortcut). Existing user-specific CRM access remains unchanged.
- Client Admin → **Customers / Excel → Site visits** can now reschedule a visit that is `requested` or `confirmed`. The chosen future date/time is saved to the client's own CRM, leaving the visit status unchanged, with an audit entry and stale-update conflict protection. Completed/cancelled visits cannot be rescheduled.
- Rescheduling does not itself make a Plivo call or send an SMS. The separate **WhatsApp** module can manage a connected client's permitted reminders; it rechecks the confirmed appointment and invalidates old scheduled reminders. See [WhatsApp follow-ups](WHATSAPP-FOLLOWUPS.md) for setup and visit/property association requirements.
- **Sales team assignment:** Client Admin can open a customer in **Customers / Excel → Customer list** and choose **Assign customer to sales staff**. Only currently active members of that company are available, and every change is audited. Assigning a customer does not give them permission to call, message or bypass consent/DND. If staff options are missing, ask AR3D Super Admin to invite members to that company first. Requires isolated Calling CRM migration `0020_lead_staff_assignments.sql`.

## Next Actions and easy reports (pre-KYC)

- Client **Company overview → View follow-ups and next actions** opens the **Results** page, showing due follow-ups and upcoming site visits from the client's own CRM.
- **Next Actions** is a read-only task reminder in the dashboard. It highlights staff follow-up dates, visit requests/confirmations, and Do Not Call entries without sending any WhatsApp/SMS or retrying a telephone call.
- **Reminder draft (Hindi/English):** For a confirmed future site visit, choose WhatsApp or SMS draft and click **Prepare reminder**, then copy the text. This requires a company admin session, cannot draft for Do Not Call/denied/blocked customers or stale/unconfirmed visits, and never invokes Plivo or changes CRM. The draft is **not an approved WhatsApp template** and no delivery/opt-in verification is implied. Sending through the separate WhatsApp module requires its own connected account, genuine permission evidence and approved template. SMS sending remains unimplemented.
- **Operations report** now shows interested leads, follow-up leads and already-due follow-ups, alongside campaign/visit counters. These are CRM statuses, not Plivo-verified answers, billed minutes or revenue.
- Future automatic callback and carrier-integrated reminders must require provider-approved routing and independently verified recipient preference and DND status.

## Queue and results

The Worker dispatches every minute within 09:00–20:00 India time. **Calling Setup → Calling capacity** sets how many calls run at once across all companies and per company; companies share it fairly (fewer live calls first, then the company served longest ago). Keep the platform number at or below the Plivo concurrency (India default 50) and the Sarvam speech-to-text plan (about one live call per 6 requests/minute: Pay-as-you-go ≈ 8, Pro ≈ 15, Growth ≈ 600). A Sarvam 429 retries that answer once and pauses new calls for two minutes. An uncertain provider outcome locks only that company until reviewed. Each scheduled run also stays inside a D1 query budget: `AR3D_DISPATCH_QUERY_BUDGET` defaults to 45 (Cloudflare Free); set it to 900 only after Workers Paid is active, which allows about 60 new calls per minute. Customers without current clearance stay queued while approved customers proceed. Explicit suppression blocks a customer. Pause or disable company calling to suspend further reservations. A call already being submitted can finish. An uncertain request locks that company's queue until operations confirms its outcome from Plivo and the application audit. The client pauses the campaign, then a current MFA Super Admin verifies the ended attempt in Plivo. After 30 minutes from submission, enter the provider evidence reference and use **Close verified attempt**. The client can then resume the remaining jobs. The closed attempt cannot be dialed again by resuming the same run.

A signed Plivo callback establishes call identity. A successful questionnaire writes tenant-scoped property details and logs one CRM audit event. Partial responses, failed transcription or a concurrently edited lead are flagged for staff follow-up. A proposed visit date is a preference. Recording begins only after pressing 1. Pressing 2 or requesting to stop a campaign call creates a permanent opt-out in the CRM. Review customer consent separately before any further contact.

For an emergency global stop, disable `AR3D_SCRIPTED_CAMPAIGNS_ENABLED`. Existing authenticated callbacks can finish recording the result of a call already in progress.
